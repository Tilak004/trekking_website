Put your trek photos here (.jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp). No restart or rebuild needed.

  hero.jpg        -> the big background photo at the top of the start page
  gallery/        -> every photo in here shows in the "From the trail" section
  anything else   -> can be chosen as a trek's cover photo in Admin > Treks > Edit

Tip: wide landscape photos work best for hero.jpg (at least 1920px wide).
Keep each photo under ~500 KB so pages load fast on mobile data.
