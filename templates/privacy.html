What we collect

When you create an account or request a booking, we collect your name, email, phone number, age, emergency contact, pickup point, and any medical or fitness notes you choose to share.

Why we collect it

  • To confirm and manage your bookings and contact you about them
  • To keep you safe on the trail. Your trek lead sees your emergency contact and medical notes
  • To show your reviews (with your name) on trek and trek lead pages

What we don't do

We don't sell your data or share it with advertisers. We only share it with the trek lead for your batch, and with authorities if required by law or in an emergency.

Cookies

We use a single cookie to keep you logged in. We don't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your data at any time by emailing us.
