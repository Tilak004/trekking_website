Cancellation & refund policy

Last updated: {{year}}

If you cancel

When you cancelRefund
7 or more days before the trekFull refund, minus a ₹200 processing fee
3 to 6 days before the trek50% refund, or move to another date for free
Less than 3 days before the trekNo refund, but you can move to another date once

Cancelling a booking request before we've confirmed it is always free. To cancel a confirmed booking, use My Bookings or message us on WhatsApp.

If we cancel

We may cancel or reschedule a trek because of heavy rain, forest department closures, unsafe conditions or too few participants. If that happens, you can choose a full refund or a free move to another date or trek.

During the trek

Your trek lead may turn the group back, or ask a participant not to continue, if conditions or someone's health make it unsafe. Once a trek has started, no refund is due in that case, but we'll always help you get back safely.

If you can't finish the trek

There is no refund, partial refund or date transfer if you leave the trek or can't complete it for your own reasons, for example:

  • you can't keep up with the group or feel the trek is too hard
  • illness, injury, dehydration or any other health problem
  • you decide to stop
  • you are asked to leave for breaking our rules, such as drinking alcohol, smoking or putting others at risk

Any extra transport, medical or evacuation costs to leave early are yours to pay. Our trek price does not include insurance; see Insurance.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval.

Questions

Message us on WhatsApp, email us or send a question and we'll get back to you.
