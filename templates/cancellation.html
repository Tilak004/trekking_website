If you cancel

When you cancelRefund
7 or more days before the trekFull refund, minus a ₹200 processing fee
3 to 6 days before the trek50% refund, or move to another date for free
Less than 3 days before the trekNo refund, but you can move to another date once

Cancelling a booking request before we've confirmed it is always free. To cancel a confirmed booking, use My Bookings or message us on WhatsApp.

If we cancel

We may cancel or reschedule a trek because of heavy rain, forest department closures, unsafe conditions or too few participants. If that happens, you can choose a full refund or a free move to another date or trek.

During the trek

Your trek lead may turn the group back, or ask a participant not to continue, if conditions or someone's health make it unsafe. No refund is due in that case, but we'll always help you get back safely.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 working days of approval.
