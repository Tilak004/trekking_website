Our story

Built by people who race mountains

One of us has finished a full Ironman. One races 24-hour adventure races and runs the Ghats most weekends. One has completed every major trek in Karnataka and crossed Hampta Pass in the Himalayas.

Between us we've seen how much a good trek lead changes the day: the right pace, a calm voice on a slippery descent, and knowing exactly where the best view is. That's what we want every trekker to get, every weekend.
